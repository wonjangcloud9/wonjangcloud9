## What I'm building now

### vibing

<p align="center">
  <img src="./assets/vibing/banner.jpg" width="100%" alt="vibing — snap your meal, see it together">
</p>

Snap a photo of what you eat and a turtle coach named **Bibi** logs the calories and protein. Bibi also plans the day's gym session — it sets the weights from your sex, muscle mass and a photo of your InBody sheet, tells you the next set while you rest, and remembers the exercises you'd rather skip. You and your friends see each other's meals. It's my eleventh weight-loss or fitness app, and the first one to reach a store.

</p>

### MetricFlow

<p align="center">
  <img src="./assets/metricflow/banner.jpg" width="100%" alt="MetricFlow — look at the numbers, fix one thing">
</p>

A growth tool for people who build products on their own. Write your goal in one sentence and it picks the five numbers worth watching, reads only as much as the data supports, and hands you one experiment for the week. When there's no data, it says so.

When I closed nanika and went through the records, I had no visitor counts per service. I couldn't say which of the eight had worked, so I started building this. I'm its first user.

</p>
